Live solver

This page runs the actual 3D solver in your browser — the same kernels as the reference implementation, ported to JavaScript and checked against it to 0.00% on rate of spread. Nothing here is interpolated or pre-computed.

It is slow. That is the point of this page — to show how slow, honestly, so the trade-offs are visible. The headline number is simulated seconds per wall-clock second: below 1 means the fire is running slower than a real one would. The solver runs in a Web Worker, so the page stays responsive while it grinds.

What you are looking at

A side-on slice through the fuel bed and the air above it, wind blowing left to right. The bright band is the flame; the plume leaning downwind above it is the solver's own solution of the momentum and buoyancy equations, not a drawn effect. Only the lowest ~1.2 m of an 8 m domain is shown — the rest is quiescent air.

Ignition is solid-phase: bed particles in the source patch are seeded hot and the gas warms through convection and pyrolysis volatiles, the way a torch-flash igniter actually works.
